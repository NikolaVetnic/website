In the fall of 2005 Milan Petrović (Brain Core, T'ong) began work on a music project that would best depict his views of the world at the time, stripped of all negative connotations and observed through emotions. From the onset the accent was on taking a new approach to music and innovating the expression, all the while striving to preserve the honesty and universality of music, which was the point of the project all along.

![HATOR – Live at Gradilište](youtube:k_eiUMAWbs4 "Live at Gradilište, Novi Sad, 10 May 2007")

Once the idea had taken form, Vladimir Miladinov (Šarlah, Skica) joined in, and together with Milan he put the finishing touches to the tracks, after which they entered the studio. A great deal of the work was done in Vladimir's Flamenco home studio, and the rest was completed at Dragan Alimpijević Pik's Digital Tone Works. The financial side of the recording was the greatest problem, half of which was covered by Vladimir's work at construction sites across Novi Sad.

![HATOR – Organic Fairytale](bandcamp:album/2573426271 "Self-released, 2007")

In early fall 2006 the recording was over, and Milan and Vladimir started thinking about where to head next. Back from summer vacation, their first step was to recruit musicians. The first to join was Bojan Adić (The Scarlet Box), followed shortly by Andrej Đorđević (Doy MeKokosh, Spiderweb). The band started rehearsing and, roughly a month and a half later, the last piece fell into place as Nikola Pacek-Vetnić joined the crew.

![HATOR live](live.webp)

![HATOR – Rehearsal at PP](youtube:huYNJ8jdKpM "Rehearsal at PP, Novi Sad, 7 May 2007")

Following a few concerts during 2007 Milan lost interest in music, and the band disbanded, with everyone involved going their separate ways.
