The SVARUN AUDIO label covers all the freelance soundtrack and stock music jobs I did over the years, mostly for personal projects but not exclusively. Apart from the K’NOSSOS and VSEVOLOD soundtracks, the music is pretty standard, run-of-the-mill cinematic fare, delivered through bombastic production and rich orchestral and synthesizer sample banks.

While writing soundtracks is not what I primarily do these days, I am still available for hire if I find the project interesting. If you think I could contribute something of value to whatever you are doing, feel free to [drop me a line](/contact).

These are watermarked versions of the tracks; the full-quality versions, without watermarks, are for sale on [AudioJungle](https://audiojungle.net/user/svarunaudio/portfolio).
