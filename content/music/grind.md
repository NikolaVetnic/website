A piece for baroque ensemble: two violins, viola, viola da gamba and harpsichord. Written for and premiered by [Belgrade Baroque](https://www.facebook.com/belgrade.baroque/) at [Culture Station Svilara](https://www.facebook.com/svilara/), Novi Sad, on 17 November 2019.
