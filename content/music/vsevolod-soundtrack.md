Initially released in 2012 along with the game, I revisited these tracks in 2017 and gave them a proper mix and master before re-releasing them online. Although they were somewhat produced in a rush – which is perhaps more audible in _Svitava_ than it is in _Underground_ – they still capture the essence of the short game they accompany rather well, and have musical qualities that recommend them for listening even apart from the game itself.

![VSEVOLOD official soundtrack](soundcloud:playlist/336182804)

It's worth mentioning that _Underground_ uses a quote from _Three Preludes_ for piano solo by Milan Mihajlović, my mentor at the Academy of Arts in Novi Sad at the time the track was written in 2012. His work greatly influenced me during my study years, so this is one of the homages I made to him in my music.
