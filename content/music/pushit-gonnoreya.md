Anton Bereš (bass, vocals), Ivan Mandić (guitars) and Nikola Vetnić (drums) started playing in the summer of 1996, in Beočin, Serbia.

## Beočin Radio Rec Sessions (1998)

![Beočin Radio Rec Sessions](bandcamp:album/585259392 "Recorded in 1998")

Recorded during the autumn of 1998 at the Beočin Radio Station, taken and mixed by Vladimir Čondrić. We were all 13 or 14 at the time. The album consists of songs dating as far back as the time we started playing, up to the moment of recording. These are our formative years, during which we first discovered and tried distortions, real drums, and, of course – booze. Thanks to Vladimir we had the privilege and honor to play "Iza zida" by Satan Panonski on the grand stage of the House of Culture in Beočin. We truly did not appreciate how good we had it back then.

## Gonnoreya (2000)

![Gonnoreya](bandcamp:album/199230776 "Recorded in the autumn of 2000")

We were all 16 at the time. The album consists mostly of songs written, practiced and performed during the criminal NATO bombardment of Yugoslavia in 1999, and represents the high point of the collective, both writing- and performance-wise. It was recorded at the band's rehearsal place at Dunavska BB, Beočin.

## B-Sides & Rare Traxx (2001–2003)

![B-Sides & Rare Traxx](bandcamp:album/3869206357 "Recorded between 2001 and 2003")

With Olja Radaković on vocals, though not everybody played on every track. Mostly experimental and joke tracks we enjoyed producing, though the listeners may not enjoy listening to the results.

## Thanks

We rehearsed at my grandparents' place, and for that reason special thanks go to the Vetnić family – Gojko, Marica, Vojislav, Jadranka and Siniša Krneta – for their support and patience during these formative years. The Pacek family as well as the Pejić family also sacrificed some of their peace and comfort in our musical advancement's favor.

What the band achieved during the turbulent 1990s in a small town in Serbia would not have been possible without the support of more experienced colleagues. We recorded on a drum set borrowed from Ivan Akmadžić and a bass borrowed from Nenad Bikar. Our first (and only, not counting live performances) video was shot and edited by Frane Malenica, who, alongside Slobodan Bob Živković, gave us some of our first music lessons. There are also our friends Ognjen Jovanović, Tihomir Aksentijević, Srđan Stevanović and Darko Ižak, from whom we borrowed tapes and gear, whose songs we sometimes covered and who we've always been eager to listen to.
