In life, when push comes to shove, people usually prefer abandoning ship to applying some extra elbow grease and making the thing work. A neutral spectator might then say that it wasn't meant to be, or that perhaps the time wasn't right. A stubborn person may then push onward regardless, only to get more and more disappointed, before finally admitting that perhaps the time was indeed not right.

Such was my experience with Lonesome Night, a band I was invited into but which then became mine before it became no one's. Perhaps we were undisciplined, perhaps we expected too much, perhaps we lacked the experience and finesse to deliver a project such as this one, but it was probably all three, and then some. Lonesome Night, the band who wanted to play serious music live but whose rehearsals always saw at least one bandmate missing and several unprepared, disbanded soon after delivering a humble promo CD, a patchwork of techniques and approaches and tracks, a monument to the Serbian proverb paraphrased as "to cut off one's ears to patch up one's ass".

![Lonesome Night – Decameron](soundcloud:playlist/1015710106 "Demo recordings, early 2020")

Fast forward from 2008 to 2019: following the disbandment of yet another SVARUN line-up I was searching for new collaborators. New challenges. A right post at a right forum – that is how I met Jon Einar Hektoen. As my backlog of never-recorded tracks and never-completed projects was bigger than I would like, I was determined not to write anything new (and hoped Jon wouldn't mind). Out of the entire offering he chose the tracks that made up Lonesome Night's never-delivered album. As I was disappointed more times than I can remember, I would contain my excitement. More so because Jon said he had a guitarist friend who would probably want to participate as well. Too good to be true.

Some time passed before either Jon or the guitarist friend wrote back. Jon then wrote and said that, due to family affairs, he would be unable to commit for some time. Used to that kind of stuff, before wrapping up the chat I mentioned the guitarist friend who never got in touch. "Odd", said Jon – in a few minutes I was talking to Mikael Aesnes Torseth.

In a calm and matter-of-fact way he explained that he would be able to commit to the project in a week or so, as it was around Christmas 2019 that we got in touch. Well, fine, I thought… Hopefully I will have a track ready before August, but even December is fine. He completed the track in three weeks.

Then another in a week.

Then a few weeks nothing (due to work), then a track and a half in one week.

And so, after being dormant for TWELVE FUCKING YEARS, the Lonesome Night project was RESURRECTED. So I guess that wasn't weird enough… How to make it WEIRDER? I know, let's record the album for such a project against the background of a CORONAVIRUS PANDEMIC while being stuck inside.

So DECAMERON… Us four – Mikael on guitars, Jon on bass, my lady Viktoriya behind the mic and myself on drums and computer – we sit in an online mansion, hiding from the fucking XXI century plague, telling stories and making stuff to pass the time. So DECAMERON. Or it will be HECTOMERON… Or KILOMERON… MEGAMERON…

We'll see ▣

## Eikosæton

The album came out on 8 May 2026: thirteen tracks, an hour and 22 minutes of music.

![Eikosæton](cover.webp "The cover of Eikosæton")

![Eikosæton](spotify:album/0Ng25z6uEdigq5ktTlLsGt "Lonesome Night, 2026")

From the album's notes:

> In 2007 I was approached by Stojan Šukunda to step in as a drummer for a band of his titled Lonesome Night, following the departure of Zoltan Simon and Boris Hložan.
>
> During 2008 the band went through a turbulent period, which culminated in an EP featuring Marko Vešović (lead guitars), Arpad More (rhythm guitars) and Đorđe Marković (vocals), besides Stojan on bass guitar and myself as composer, lyricist and drummer. As we were readying ourselves for the album recording sessions, the entire project went on a hiatus and the album, which was almost completed, was shelved.
>
> In 2020 the project was revived by the efforts of Mikael Aesnes Torseth, Jon Einar Hektoen, Viktorija Karhina and myself. What you have before you is the result of this 20-year-long journey. We sincerely hope you like it.

### Recordings

All vocal tracks were recorded by Valerian Buila during the summer and autumn of 2022 at his home studio in Novi Sad, Serbia. All guitar tracks were recorded by the performing musicians in their own home studios between 2020 and 2023. Some drum tracks were recorded by Nikola Pacek-Vetnić during home rehearsals held during 2021 on a Roland V-Drums kit.

Edited, mixed and mastered by Nikola Pacek-Vetnić between 2020 and 2023.
