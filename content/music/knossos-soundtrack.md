> "…Finally, there is also positive feedback in regards to the audio of the game – it seems that experimental music is not that scary once put into a broader context (which is a revelation for me, to be honest)." (from the interview given to [Indie Graze](https://www.indiegraze.com/2017/10/01/interview-knossos-nikola-vetnic/))

Even a casual glance at the art of K’NOSSOS gives off a vibe of an amalgam of various disjointed elements, brought together by means of palette and general art direction rooted in the works of Kandinsky. The soundtrack is built around the same principles, using recorded samples, classical instruments and amplified guitars to create a soundscape which, in the author's view, brings to mind images of classical antiquity blended into desolate visions of abandoned space stations and vast space-faring vessels.

![K’NOSSOS official soundtrack](soundcloud:playlist/332651723)

Just as the game itself, this soundtrack is a work in progress. On its own the music is perhaps a bit out of context – to put things into proper perspective, have a look at [the game](/games/knossos).
