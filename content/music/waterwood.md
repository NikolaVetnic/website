Waterwood was founded in December 2014 in Novi Sad as an unplugged project performing original music. The line-up included Miloš Aničić (Taboo) from Bor (Serbia), Kirill Boyko (Olympiada, Burek) from St Petersburg (Russia) and Vladimir Chudinov (Acoustic Pleasure, Starlite Band) from Novosibirsk (Russia). During the first six months some twenty tracks were written, joining them as a music collective. While rehearsing, they were on the lookout for a rhythm section.

![Waterwood – Live at CK13](soundcloud:playlist/334289683 "Live at CK13, Novi Sad, 2015")

In July 2015 Waterwood was joined by Marko Golik (The Workers), also from Bor, and three months later the line-up was completed with Nikola Pacek-Vetnić from Novi Sad.

![Waterwood](band.webp)

![Waterwood – Open Road](youtube:Lcs4C25iTCw "Open Road, live at CK13")

The band first performed live together in December 2015 and was fairly active during the next twelve months. In the fall of 2016 the band effectively disbanded, as the recording ran into difficulties, and everyone went on to pursue their own projects.
