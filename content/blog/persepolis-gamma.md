_Originally written in February 2010. as a required piece for chamber string ensemble during my 3rd year as a student of undergraduate composition program under mentorship of professor Milan Mihajlović. Revised for the guitar ensemble during recording sessions in 2019. Edited, mixed and mastered by Nikola Pacek-Vetnić between June 2019 and February 2020._
