He currently works as a software developer and AI engineer at Brighting, a Dutch-Serbian company. Primarily interested in the governance and safe use of AI, game development and computer graphics. A full IT-related resume is available [here](/about/resume).

He is blessed with a wonderful wife and two very energetic sons.
