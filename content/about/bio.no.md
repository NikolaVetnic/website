_Nikola Vetnić_ (1984) completed his undergraduate and master studies in computer science and informational technology at the University of Novi Sad (Serbia), as well as obtained his doctorate in music composition at the University of Arts in Belgrade (Serbia).

He is currently working full time as a lead developer for the Rationale Information Technologies based in Novi Sad. Primarily interested in full stack web apps, computer graphics and game development. My [full IT related resume](/about/resume) is [available here](/about/resume).

Blessed with a wonderful wife and two very energetic sons.
